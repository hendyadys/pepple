ac cell (+ mitosis cell) thought experiments
1. probabilistic labels
  a. instead of deterministic
2. can't just feed images in naive probabilistic way
  a. imagine a cell, labelled by one grader but not other
  b. and all your data consists of that one cell
  c. then creating 2 copies of image focused in on cell, one with label and one without label
  d. dl wouldnt learn anything as gradients would cancel!
3. what if every cell label was probabilistic?
  a. if cell labelled by both, sample from a distribution 2p/(2p+epsilon), and label according to sample
  b. augment and include in training
  c. if cell labelled by one, sample from a distribution p/(2p+epsilon)
  d. sample in this ratio or would this still confuse dl?
4. this is similar in spirit to label smoothing, as noise in labels
5. alternate is mixup (https://arxiv.org/pdf/1710.09412) and cutMix (https://arxiv.org/pdf/1905.04899.pdf)
  a. but these focus on classification
  b. cutmix overlays different images together with geometry and smoothing
  c. maybe we can revisit accell by seeding cells and smoothing
    i. grab a larger patch instead of focused in on cell and apply smoothing
    ii. this maybe more robust as dl doesn't focus too much on seeded cell boundaries!
  d. NB - accell dimensions (~5*5 pixels) much smaller than image dimensions (1024*1000), so downsampling isnt really an option

  c. fpn (feature pyramid instead image pyramid), https://arxiv.org/pdf/1612.03144.pdf
  d. ssd (1-stage), https://arxiv.org/pdf/1512.02325.pdf
  e. focal loss (helps 1-stage detectors to avoid FP!), https://arxiv.org/pdf/1708.02002.pdf
